Sarkari Kam Ki Zaruri Websites

Niche di gayi list se aap kisi bhi sarkari website par direct ja sakte hain:

  • Aadhaar Card Portal (UIDAI)

    Kis kaam aata hai: Naya Aadhaar Card banana, Aadhaar update karna, aur status check karna.


  • Voter ID Portal (Election Commission)

    Kis kaam aata hai: Naya Voter ID Card apply karna, Voter ID download karna, aur Voter List mein naam dekhna.


  • PAN Card / Income Tax Portal

    Kis kaam aata hai: Naya PAN Card banana, PAN-Aadhaar Link karna, aur Income Tax Return (ITR) bharna.


  • Parivahan Sewa Portal

    Kis kaam aata hai: Driving License (DL) apply karna, Learning License test dena, aur Vehicle RC ki jankari nikalna.


  • Passport Seva Portal

    Kis kaam aata hai: Naya Passport apply karna, appointment book karna, aur Passport application ka status track karna.


  • PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Portal

    Kis kaam aata hai: Kisan kist ka status check karna, e-KYC karna, aur naye kisan ka registration karna.


  • Sarkari Result / Sarkari Naukri

    Kis kaam aata hai: Nayi Sarkari Jobs, Admit Cards, Exam Dates, aur Exam Results dekhna.

Note: Kisi bhi link par click karte hi website naye tab mein khul jayegi.
